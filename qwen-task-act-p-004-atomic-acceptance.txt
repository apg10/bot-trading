GOAL
Gate every new PAPER BUY LIMIT/MARKET acceptance with the accepted pure risk and
symbol-filter evaluators inside the same SQLite write transaction that creates
the reservation and changes PREPARED to ACCEPTED. A stale/precomputed reservation
total must never let two concurrent BUYs jointly exceed `max_total_exposure`.

CONTRACT
1. Extend `SQLiteStore.accept_paper_order` with explicit, separately supplied
   risk policy, `PaperRiskSnapshot`, `SymbolFilterSnapshot`, and optional explicit
   market notional reference(s). None means absent, not a default. For a NEW BUY,
   missing required risk/filter context rejects fail-closed. Keep StrategyConfig
   unchanged. Do not fetch Binance or any other network data.
2. Within `_accept_paper_order_inner`'s existing `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` transaction,
   load/validate the persisted PREPARED order and current book. Recompute the sum
   of active BUY quote reservations from durable rows in that transaction; do NOT
   trust caller `remaining_buy_reservations`. Build a validated risk snapshot
   using that current sum and the caller's explicit equity/marked-inventory and
   market references. Keep current available-balance and reservation calculations
   authoritative and transaction-local.
3. Evaluate `evaluate_paper_risk` and `validate_symbol_filters` before the first
   write. Risk policy/snapshot and filter snapshot must match the persisted order;
   pass explicit market reference(s) unchanged. No quantity/price rounding or
   silent reduction. On any gate rejection, raise a typed acceptance-gate error
   carrying the stable code and exact result references; transaction leaves the
   order PREPARED and makes no balance/reservation/acceptance writes.
4. BUY acceptance writes the accepted order, reservation, balance, and an
   immutable gate receipt atomically. Store only stable result codes and policy,
   risk-snapshot, filter-snapshot/source, and used market-reference identifiers/
   versions/timestamps; do not persist configured limit values or market prices.
   Version the JSON acceptance payload to v2. Continue to validate/read existing
   v1 acceptance payloads unchanged; no SQL table migration is needed or allowed.
5. For successful BUYs, persist `risk_approved` plus policy id/version and
   `symbol_filter_approved` plus snapshot/filter-set/source refs. For rejection,
   expose the evaluator result through the typed error; create no acceptance row.
6. Preserve idempotency: an already accepted order returns its current order and
   stored receipt without re-evaluating, reserving again, or requiring new gate
   inputs. Preserve legacy v1 stored records on load/reopen.
7. SELL paths remain as currently scoped: risk/filter validators return
   not-applicable; they must not block SELL acceptance or cancellations. Preserve
   explicit SELL lot reservations and all existing order/fill behavior.
8. In the same transaction, the second of two different concurrent BUY accepts
   must observe the first BUY's committed quote reservation when recomputing
   exposure. Test two otherwise-valid orders whose combined exposure breaches
   the fixture policy: at most one may be accepted; the other remains PREPARED,
   with balances/reservations consistent. Also test a combined exposure exactly
   at the limit according to the approved `<=` rule.
